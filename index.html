Rate curves

Betaflight and Actual, using the Velocidrone formulas.

Matches center and max, then fits the shape. Values snap to steps you can enter.

Formulas

Stick s runs from −1 to 1. That is the deflection from center, divided by 500 µs. Both curves are odd: left stick mirrors the right.

Betaflight

Rc expo reshapes the stick, then Rc rate scales it. Rate divides by a shrinking number, which bends the curve even when expo is 0. The divisor uses the raw stick, not the expo-shaped one.

shaped = s·|s|³·expo + s·(1 − expo)
K = 200 · R
R = rc rate, or rc rate + 14.54·(rc rate − 2) when rc rate > 2
deg/s = K · shaped / clamp(1 − |s|·rate, 0.01, 1)

Center, the slope at mid-stick, is K·(1 − expo). Max, at full stick, is K / clamp(1 − rate, 0.01, 1). Expo does not change the max.

Actual

Center is that same slope, in deg/s. Max rate is full stick. Expo mixes a square term with a sixth-power term, which holds the middle down.

g = |s| · (s⁵·expo + s·(1 − expo))
deg/s = s·center + max(0, max − center) · g

Conversion

Betaflight to Actual keeps the measured center and max, then picks the expo with the smallest error. Actual to Betaflight keeps that center and max, then searches the mix of Rc rate, Rate, and Rc expo. The curves are different families, so a few deg/s of error remain. The flight code also clamps the setpoint at ±1998 deg/s. The graph draws the unclamped shape and marks that line when a curve crosses it.
